# Customer email templates

These are operational templates for a future connected order workflow. They are not sent by the static website prototype.

## Request received

**Subject:** We got your request, [name]

Hi [name],

Thanks for sending [character name] our way. [Artist] is reading your notes now and will reply within [24 hours] with a quote and a delivery date.

You do not pay anything until you are happy with both.

Refpaw

Price: $[amount]  
Delivery: [date]  
Includes: [list]

To go ahead, pay the deposit of $[amount] here: [link]. I will send the sketch by [date].

[Artist]
